# Code of Conduct

GlossWise contributors are expected to keep project spaces welcoming,
constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Give specific, actionable technical feedback.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about safety and correctness.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or confidential information.
- Help maintain a harassment-free environment.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and sustained
personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
For security-sensitive reports, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers
may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces representing GlossWise.
